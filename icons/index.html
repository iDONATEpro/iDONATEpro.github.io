For developers

Same files on the public site and in the CRM. Not Lucide. Copy the files. Do not restyle, offset, or hotlink.

  • Same-origin sprite only. Browsers will not paint <use> from another domain. Do not hotlink idonatepro.github.io from the app.
  • Draw 32–40px inside a 48–56px tile. Color with CSS via currentColor.
  • Bump ?v= on idp.svg whenever the sprite changes. Slugs are the <symbol id>s (home, save, data-services…).
